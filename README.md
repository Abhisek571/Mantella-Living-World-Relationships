# Mantella Living World - Relationships

Mantella NPCs who know where they stand with you. A module of [Mantella Living World - Integration Hub](https://github.com/Abhisek571/Mantella-Living-World-Hub).

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| Marriage (M.A.R.A.S) | Status lines, spouses and co-spouses. Proposal odds are shown only for NPCs M.A.R.A.S tracks as proposal candidates, as a six-step scale, and never for followers who marry through their own quest. M.A.R.A.S's own chance decides a proposal: rolled once per NPC per game day, so asking again the same day gets the same answer. No proposal is heard for 7 game days after a divorce or a called-off engagement. Actions: accept a proposal, break off an engagement, divorce, settle a marriage crisis, change affection. On M.A.R.A.S 0.11.1 a second engagement needs its polygamy (MCM toggle or its polygamy quest). When a step cannot happen, the talk is told that nothing has changed. Generic (non-unique) spouses, such as a married guard, get the status line but no Mantella marriage actions: every actor of that kind shares one file. Same-sex courtship needs M.A.R.A.S MCM > Show start dialogue to genders set to All (on 0.11.1 an NPC of your sex is told once why they cannot be courted) |
| Affection (M.A.R.A.S) | The NPC can raise or lower their own M.A.R.A.S affection score from what is said in a talk: a little (1) or a lot (3), at most 5 up or down per game day. It never drops the score below 25, so a talk alone cannot start a marriage crisis, and it does nothing during one |
| Event points (M.A.R.A.S) | Other modules' score events (World: deeds, care, gifts) move the score silently, scaled by what wins that NPC over in the Hub (rated 1 x0.5, 2 x1, 3 x1.5), rounded, so a small gift can count nothing. Each kind counts at most once a game hour per NPC and shares the daily limit and the floor at 25 with the action. Physical closeness is left to M.A.R.A.S when it counts scenes itself: on 2.x, or on 0.11.1 with Lover's Ledger |
| Wedding day (M.A.R.A.S) | A spouse knows the wedding date and how long you have been married. Only for weddings that happen with this module installed |
| Yes/No box | Each marriage action asks the player first. Allowed once per game day per NPC and per kind of action; choosing No also blocks a retry for that day, and the talk is told that you said no and nothing changed |
| Learn the player's spouses | Lets the player tell an NPC who they are married and engaged to. Only offered when M.A.R.A.S is installed |
| Marriage crisis | When the spouse joins a talk, the crisis moves to M.A.R.A.S's talk stage, as its own dialogue does, and the NPC is told to decide in the talk. M.A.R.A.S ends the crisis in divorce after 3 game days. The plain divorce action is not offered while a crisis runs. On M.A.R.A.S 2.x with its divorce confirmation on, only its box asks |
| Family ties (Relations Finder) | Parents, children, siblings, spouses, cousins, partners, bosses, employees, enemies and sworn enemies from the game's own relationship records. Up to 4 names per kind; dead relatives are marked. For an NPC engaged or married to the player through M.A.R.A.S, the game's own spouse or partner is named as former |
| Shared history (Lover's Ledger) | A short summary of what you have been through together (a few favourite acts and a few other lovers, group scenes noted) and a short talk after a scene. None of it is told while a child is in the talk. Needs an adult framework that Lover's Ledger supports (OStim). The scene line goes only to a talk the player is in (with OStim Expansion installed, it tells that talk itself) or to the after-scene talk. The after-scene talk is skipped if the partner is dead or in combat; `set MRB_AfterSceneTalk to 0` in the console turns it off (1 turns it back on) |
| Closeness | Engaged and married NPCs count as close for the other modules' actions; on M.A.R.A.S 2.x, also dating and lover |
| M.A.R.A.S 2.x | Dating, lover, ex-dating and ex-lover lines. A Mantella talk counts as attention |

Each part runs only when its mod is installed. Group talks: if the last speaker cannot take a marriage step, the action goes to the one NPC in the talk who can; with two or more possible, nothing happens. A child, or an NPC with a M.A.R.A.S status (spouse, fiance, ex), always speaks for themselves and is never swapped for another. An affection change is always the speaker's own. Radiant talks between NPCs get none of these lines. Without Mantella's Advanced Actions on (Actions tab of Mantella's settings), the actions are not offered.

Family (with Relations Finder): each NPC hears their family and ties. With Mantella Living World - World installed, a relative who died during the playthrough gets the day, close family (parent, child, sibling, spouse, partner) who died in the last 30 days are grieved, and close family you struck in that time are mentioned.

Requires: the Integration Hub, Mantella - Direct AI Connect (supplies the action events), [Mantella](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/98631) 0.14 with Advanced Actions on, [Papyrus MessageBox - SKSE NG](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/83578), PapyrusUtil SE, SKSE_HTTP (comes with Mantella). Optional, each for its own part: [M.A.R.A.S](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/159033) (with its TT_MARAS.esp), [Lover's Ledger](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/158983), [Relations Finder](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/158980). Keep the Hub and this module on matching versions.

Build: the Hub's `tools/build.py --only relationships`, then `tools/compile_maras2x.py` for the M.A.R.A.S 2.x script.

M.A.R.A.S 2.x is recognised by its loose `Scripts/TTM_Actions.pex`. A 2.x build that ships its scripts only inside a BSA is taken for 0.11.1.
