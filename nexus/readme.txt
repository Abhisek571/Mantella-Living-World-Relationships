Mantella Living World - Relationships
Version 1.0.0 by itsPigeon2000

Mantella NPCs who know where they stand with you: marriage and spouses (M.A.R.A.S) and your history together (Lover's Ledger). Each part runs only when its mod is installed.

Requirements
- Mantella Living World - Integration Hub
- Mantella
- Either or both: M.A.R.A.S, Lover's Ledger

Installation
1. Install the hub, then this module.
